  - K719: If you can speak so that a good assembly takes it in, don’t speak in a petty one, even by forgetting.
  - K714: Be bright before the bright. Before the blank, take on the colour of white lime.

## Success is going to your head

- **Can feel like Elation:** When things go well, high spirits can start to feel like strength.
- **Humility:** Says plenty is the time to bow low, and that in the rich humility is a further wealth.
  - K963: In plenty, bow low. When your means shrink, stand tall.
  - K125: Humility is good in everyone, but in the rich it is a further wealth.
- **Vigilance:** Says that when delight makes you feel strong, remember those who were ruined by neglect.
  - K539: When your delight makes you feel strong, remember those who were ruined by neglect.
- **Greatness:** Says prosperity has greatness in it only for those without pride, anger or pettiness.
  - K431: Those without pride, anger or pettiness have a prosperity with greatness in it.

## You are spending more than you earn

- **Can feel like Worry:** Watching money go faster than it comes can sit at the back of your mind all day.
- **Moderation:** Says a narrow way in does no harm as long as the way out is no wider, and that knowing your limit is how to look after wealth as you spend it.
  - K478: The way in may be narrow, but there is no harm, as long as the way out is no wider.
  - K477: Give in the right way, knowing your limit. That is how to look after your wealth as you spend it.
- **What the Kural doesn’t offer:** It speaks of keeping what goes out within what comes in. It has nothing on debt you already owe, or on how to get clear of it.

## You are struggling to make ends meet

- **Can feel like Worry:** When money is short, tomorrow’s needs can be hard to put out of your mind, even at night.
- **Can feel like Humiliation:** Having little can bring the feeling of being looked down on, even by people who mean no harm.
- **Worth:** Says having nothing is no disgrace if you have the strength called worth, and that when your means shrink you should stand tall.
  - K988: Having nothing is no disgrace, if you have the strength called worth.
  - K963: In plenty, bow low. When your means shrink, stand tall.
- **Exertion:** Says nothing is sweeter than eating what your own labour has brought, even gruel as thin as water.
  - K1065: Even if it is gruel as thin as water, nothing is sweeter than eating what your own labour has brought.
- **Compassion:** Says those who lose wealth may flower again one day, and counts losing compassion as the worse loss.
  - K248: Those who lose wealth may flower again one day. Those who lose compassion are lost, and will hardly rise.
- **What the Kural doesn’t offer:** It offers no practical way out. It says only poverty hurts like poverty, that there is no sleep in it, and that many miseries settle there. Some lines on the poor are harsh. parimēlaḻagar says a body should be kept, even by begging.

## You have to ask someone for money

- **Can feel like Humiliation:** Having to ask can feel like being lowered, even before anyone has answered.
- **Can feel like Worry:** Not knowing what the answer will be, or what you will do without it, can stay with you.
- **Begging:** Says to ask those it is right to ask, not those who hide what they have, and that if they hide it the blame is theirs, not yours.
  - K1051: If you see someone it is right to beg from, beg. If they hide what they have, the blame is theirs, not yours.
  - K1067: I beg of everyone who begs: if you must beg, don’t beg from those who hide what they have.
- **Worth:** Says having nothing is no disgrace, if you have the strength called worth.
  - K988: Having nothing is no disgrace, if you have the strength called worth.
- **What the Kural doesn’t offer:** This is never made easy. Chapter 106 says you may ask those who give gladly. Chapter 107 dreads the asking itself, even from them. parimēlaḻagar says a body should be kept, even this way. It offers little for a refusal.

## Someone in need asks you for help

- **Can feel like Restlessness:** Wanting to help and not being sure you can may keep you from settling.
- **Charity:** Says being asked is painful until you see the pleased face of the one who asked, and that those who give without scorn gladden the asker’s heart.
  - K224: Being begged from is painful, until you see the pleased face of the one who begged.
  - K1057: On seeing those who give without scorn or insult, the heart is glad and rejoices deep within.
- **Moderation:** Warns too that helping without weighing what you have will quickly destroy your wealth.
  - K480: Helping others without weighing what you have will quickly destroy your wealth.
  - K477: Give in the right way, knowing your limit. That is how to look after your wealth as you spend it.

## You have money and it brings you nothing

- **Can feel like Loneliness:** Having money and no one to share it with can feel lonely.
- **Can feel like Restlessness:** Having enough and still not being at ease can keep the mind turning.
- **Charity:** Asks whether those who hoard know the joy of giving and being glad, and says ending hunger is where wealth can be stored.
  - K228: Do they not know the joy of giving and being glad, those hard-hearted people who hoard what they own and lose it?
  - K226: End the ruinous hunger of those who have nothing. That is where someone who has wealth can store it.
- **Cherishing Kin:** Says the reward of wealth is to live so that your kin gather round you, and that a life with no sharing of hearts is a tank with no bank.
  - K524: The reward of having wealth is to live so that your kin gather round you.
  - K523: A life with no sharing of hearts is like a broad tank filling with water and no bank around it.
- **Helpfulness:** Says the wealth of one who loves the world is like the village tank filled with water.
  - K215: The wealth of a person of great understanding who loves the world is like the village tank filled with water.
- **What the Kural doesn’t offer:** It judges a life like this only from outside, and harshly. It doesn’t describe how it feels from within, or say the feeling will lift. What it does offer is giving and sharing, which it calls a joy.

## Your drinking is getting out of hand

- **Can feel like Craving:** Drink can come to feel needed, even when you can see what it costs.
- **Can feel like Guilt:** Drinking in secret, or after promising not to, can leave your heart sinking.
- **Self-Examination:** Asks the drinker, sober for a moment, to see a drunk man and think of his own slips, and to give up saying he has never been drunk.
  - K930: When a drinker, sober for the moment, sees a drunk man, doesn’t he think of how he slips when he has drunk?
  - K928: Give up saying, “I have never been drunk.” What you hid in your heart will only show the more, then and there.
- **What the Kural doesn’t offer:** It offers no cure and no way of stopping. It condemns: liquor costs you your wealth, your glory and your senses, and those around you see through it. It doesn’t say what to do when you can’t stop.
- Also K925: Paying out your wealth to buy senselessness shows you don’t know what to do.

## You cannot stop gambling

- **Can feel like Craving:** Each loss can make the next bet feel more necessary, not less.
- **Can feel like Guilt:** What has been lost, and who doesn’t know yet, can weigh on you.
- **Understanding:** Its one counsel is to see the win for what it is, a hook swallowed by a fish, and to see your wealth and income going to others.
  - K931: Don’t want gambling, even if you win. Your winnings are a hook swallowed by a fish.
  - K933: Keep on calling the throws of the rolling dice, and your wealth and income will leave and go to others.
- **What the Kural doesn’t offer:** It offers no cure. It says the win is a hook, that the love of gambling grows with each loss, and that it takes your wealth, your good name and your compassion. It doesn’t say how to stop.
- Also K940: As gambling is loved more with each loss, so the soul loves more with each pain.

## Your eating is harming your health

- **Can feel like Worry:** Seeing your health change can stay on your mind.
- **Can feel like Guilt:** Eating against what you know is good for you can sit badly afterwards.
- **Health:** Says to eat knowing the measure once your food is digested, and that too much or too little both bring disease.
  - K943: When your food has been digested, eat knowing the measure. That is how someone who has a body makes it last.
  - K941: Too much or too little, and the three the learned list, starting with wind, will cause disease.
- **What the Kural doesn’t offer:** It speaks of measure, and mostly of eating too much. It says too little also brings disease, but it has nothing for someone who can’t control their eating.

## However much you have, you want more

- **Can feel like Craving:** Each new thing can leave you already wanting the next.
- **Desirelessness:** Says craving’s nature is never to be filled, and that where it exists, suffering comes on and on.
  - K368: Those without craving have no suffering. Where it exists, suffering comes on and on, without end.
  - K370: If you give up craving, whose nature is never to be filled, that very moment gives you a nature that never changes.
- **Righteousness (via Craving):** Fearing craving is called righteousness, and craving is one of four things righteousness is free of.
  - K366: Craving is what deceives you. To fear it is righteousness.
  - K35: Envy, craving, anger and harsh words: righteousness is a life free of these four.

## Wondering whether to stop eating meat

- **Can feel like Guilt:** Thinking about what the animal went through can sit uneasily with you.
- **Abstaining from Meat:** Says no one would offer flesh for money if the world didn’t kill to eat, and that flesh is another creature’s wound.
  - K256: If the world didn’t kill in order to eat, no one would offer flesh for money.
  - K257: Flesh is another creature’s wound. If you know that, you must not eat it.
- **Compassion:** Asks how someone who eats other flesh to swell their own can hold to compassion.
  - K251: How can someone who eats other flesh to swell their own hold to compassion?
  - K254: What is compassion? Not killing. What is not? Killing. Eating that flesh is worthless.

## Wanting to do right by your children

- **Can feel like Worry:** Wanting to get it right for your children can keep you turning things over.
- **Offspring:** Says the good a father does his son is to set him in the front rank, and that children who know more than you are sweet.
  - K67: The good a father does his son is to put him in the front rank of the assembly.
  - K68: That your children know more than you is sweet to every living being on this great earth.

## You have fallen out with a relative

- **Can feel like Hurt:** A break with your own family can hurt in a way that is hard to explain to others.
- **Can feel like Anger:** Old grievances can come back along with the new one.
- **Cherishing Kin:** Says those who left will come back as kin once the cause of the rift is gone, and that your own people’s ways are bad for you if they do you harm.
  - K529: Those who were your own and left you will come back as kin, once the cause of the rift is gone.
  - K881: Shade and water are bad for you if they do harm. Your own people’s ways are bad too, if they do you harm.
- Also K530: When someone who left comes back for a reason, a ruler should meet that reason, think it through and take them in.

## You are carrying the whole family on your shoulders

- **Can feel like Losing Heart:** Being the one everyone leans on can wear your spirit down over time.
- **Can feel like Worry:** With others depending on you, it can be hard to stop thinking about what comes next.
- **The Family Line:** Says the load falls on those who can carry it, and asks, for them, whether such a body is only a vessel for suffering.
  - K1027: As the battlefield rests on the fearless, among your own people too the load falls on those who can carry it.
  - K1029: Is the body of the man who shields his family from harm only a vessel for suffering?
- **What the Kural doesn’t offer:** It offers no rest, and it doesn’t say how to set the load down. It honours the one who carries and sees what the carrying costs, and it goes no further.

## You have just fallen for someone

- **Can feel like Being Smitten:** Someone can strike you all at once, before you know anything about them.
- **Can feel like Desire:** Thoughts of them can arrive without being asked.
- **The Signs of Love:** Part 3 watches for a look that is returned: she looks at the ground, and looks at him when he looks away.
  - K1094: When I look at her, she looks at the ground. When I don’t, she looks at me and softly smiles.
- **What the Kural doesn’t offer:** It gives no advice here. Part 3 only sings what a lover feels, and the looks of two people who both want this. It isn’t a way of reading someone who hasn’t said so.

## People are gossiping about your relationship

- **Can feel like Hurt:** Hearing your private life passed around can hurt, even if it is only talk.
- **Can feel like Worry:** You may keep wondering who knows, and what they will do.
- **Gossip About Lovers:** Part 3 says such talk doesn’t end desire but feeds it, as ghee feeds a fire.
  - K1148: To say you will put out desire with gossip is like saying you will put out a fire with ghee.
  - K1147: The town’s gossip is the manure and my mother’s words the water, and this sickness grows on.
- **What the Kural doesn’t offer:** It doesn’t say how to stop the talk, or what to do if it puts you at risk. Part 3 only shows such talk feeding desire and not ending it.

## Your partner is away for a long time

- **Can feel like Pining:** A long absence can wear on you, often worst in the evenings.
- **Can feel like Longing to Meet:** You may find yourself counting the days until they are back.
- **Can feel like Loneliness:** The nights can feel long with no one there.
- **Loving Remembrance:** Says that when you think of the one you love, no pain comes.
  - K1202: Desire is sweet in every way. When you think of the one you love, no pain comes.
- **What the Kural doesn’t offer:** Its only comfort is memory, and it admits that can hurt too: “even remembering burns my heart.” It doesn’t shorten the wait.

## You love someone who doesn't love you back

- **Can feel like One-Sided Love:** Loving someone who doesn’t answer can be a weight you carry alone.
- **Can feel like Hurt:** Not being loved back can hurt, even when no one has done anything wrong.
- **Reserve:** Thinking of sending him word, she tells her heart that telling her pain to one who has drawn away is like trying to fill in the sea.
  - K1200: Long may you live, my heart. You would tell your sickness to one who isn’t close to you. Fill in the sea instead.
- **What the Kural doesn’t offer:** Part 3 speaks in the lover’s own voice. It knows this pain, and that desire on one side only is like a load on one end of a pole. It offers no cure, and it doesn’t say how the pain ends.
- Also K1195: What can the one I love do for me, if he doesn’t love me too?
- Also K1196: Desire on one side only is painful. Like the load on a carrying pole, it is sweet when it is on both sides.

## You can't let go of someone who has left you

- **Can feel like Pining:** Someone gone can stay with you all day, and the mind can keep going back.
- **Can feel like Loving Remembrance:** Memory can keep you going and burn at the same time.
- **Reserve:** She tells her own heart that keeping in it one who left only costs her more, and that going after him is foolish.
  - K1250: If we keep in our heart the one who left us and won’t come near, we will lose still more of our beauty.
  - K1248: You pine because he won’t show tender kindness, and you go after the one who left. You are a fool, my heart.
- **What the Kural doesn’t offer:** It doesn’t say how to stop. She tells her heart it is foolish, and in the next chapter her reserve breaks down. This is a woman left by a living lover, and it has no word for someone lost to death.
- Also K1243: Why sit here thinking of him and aching, heart? The one who gave us this sickness of grief has no tender thought for us.

## You have had a fight with your partner

- **Can feel like Hurt:** A fight with the person closest to you can hurt more than the words alone.
- **Can feel like Anger:** In a fight, anger can feel like the only way to be heard.
- **Sulking:** Her friend, come as his go-between, likens a sulk to salt, good in the right amount and spoiled by drawing it out. It is called sweet only with those who love you.
  - K1302: A sulk is like the right amount of salt. Drawing it out is like a little too much.
  - K1309: Water is sweet in the shade, and sulking is sweet with those who love you.
- **What the Kural doesn’t offer:** All this is about a lovers’ sulk between two people who love each other, and the friend is speaking for him. It says nothing about a partner who frightens or harms you, and it doesn’t ask you to give way to one.

## Your partner reads betrayal into everything

- **Can feel like Hurt:** Being doubted over small things, when you have done nothing, can hurt.
- **Can feel like Fear:** Being suspected all the time can make a person careful of every word.
- **What the Kural doesn’t offer:** Chapter 132 shows this only as love play: she has no real cause, and her love runs over. It offers no way through, and nothing for suspicion that frightens or controls you. Don’t take it as a reason to bear that.

## Thinking about how short life is

- **Can feel like Fear:** Thinking about how little time there is can bring fear, at any age.
- **Can feel like Restlessness:** The thought can come back at night and keep the mind from settling.
- **Righteousness:** Its answer to how short life is: do good now, for righteousness is the companion that doesn’t die when you die.
  - K335: Before your tongue is stilled and the hiccup rises, rush to do good deeds.
  - K36: Don’t say “I’ll see to it some other day.” Practise righteousness, for it is the companion that doesn’t die when you die.
- **Impermanence:** Says no one can be sure of one moment more, so do at once the things that stay.
  - K337: They can’t be sure of living one moment more, yet they think thoughts by the ten million, and more.
  - K333: It is the nature of wealth not to stay. When you get it, do at once the things that stay.
- **What the Kural doesn’t offer:** It doesn’t console. It says life is uncertain and asks you to do good while you can. It has no word for mourning someone, and nothing here is about ending your own life.

## You want to walk away from it all

- **Can feel like Losing Heart:** Being worn down can make you want to set everything down.
- **Can feel like Restlessness:** The thought of leaving can keep coming back and won’t let the mind settle.
- **Friendship:** Says to keep close those who can end a suffering that has come, and that friendship shares the pain when ruin comes.
  - K442: Cherish and keep close those who can end the suffering that has come and guard against it before it comes.
  - K787: Friendship turns you from what would ruin you, sets you on the path, and shares the pain when ruin comes.
- **Family Life:** Says a home life lived in righteousness, with loving-kindness in it, has nothing to gain from leaving for a renouncer’s path.
  - K46: If you live your home life in the way of righteousness, what would you gain by going off on some outside path?
  - K45: If home life has loving-kindness and righteousness, that is its quality and its fruit.
- **What the Kural doesn’t offer:** It doesn’t settle this. One chapter praises letting go of attachment, another a home lived rightly, not one where you are hurt. Letting go there means a way of living, not an end to life. It has little for someone simply worn out.

## It feels as if fate is against you

- **Can feel like Helplessness:** When one thing after another goes wrong, it can seem that nothing you do will matter.
- **Can feel like Losing Heart:** A long run of bad luck can wear down the will to try again.
- **Exertion:** Says effort pays the wages of your toil even where the result is denied, and that those who don’t falter will see the back of fate.
  - K619: Even if god won’t allow it, effort pays the wages of your body’s toil.
  - K620: Those who strive without faltering and without delay will see the back of fate itself.
- **Fate:** Says the lack of a favouring fate disgraces no one, and that in making money what should help can go wrong and what should hurt can turn out well.
  - K618: The lack of a favouring fate disgraces no one. The disgrace is to know what must be known and make no effort.
  - K375: When making money, everything that should help can go wrong, and everything that should hurt can turn out well.
- **What the Kural doesn’t offer:** It says nothing is stronger than fate, and also that those who don’t falter will see its back, and it leaves the two side by side. It has little for someone worn out. Its fate is about fortune, not about what someone else does to you.

## Your kindness is not being returned

- **Can feel like Hurt:** Giving and getting nothing back can hurt, most of all from those you helped.
- **Can feel like Losing Heart:** After a long time of giving, it can be hard to see why you go on.
- **Helpfulness:** Says help given with no thought of return is greater than the sea, and that no one repays the rain.
  - K103: Weigh the goodness of help given with no thought of return, and it is greater than the sea.
  - K211: Duty asks for no return. What does the world give back to the rain?
- **Moderation:** Warns too that helping others without weighing what you have will quickly destroy your wealth.
  - K480: Helping others without weighing what you have will quickly destroy your wealth.
- **What the Kural doesn’t offer:** It doesn’t promise thanks, and it pulls two ways: help looks for no return, yet helping past what you have ruins you. It doesn’t say when to stop, and it has nothing for being used.

## Someone helped you when you most needed it

- **Can feel like Worry:** You may wonder how you could ever repay it.
- **Gratitude:** Says a good deed done at the right time, though small, is greater than the world, and that those who know its worth hold it so.
  - K102: A good deed done at the right time, though small, is far greater than the world.
  - K104: Though the good done them is the size of a millet seed, those who know its worth take it as the size of a palmyra.
- **What the Kural doesn’t offer:** These lines are about what you feel you owe. They don’t say what a helper may ask of you, and they aren’t a reason to stay with someone who uses past help to control you.
- Also K107: Through seven lives and seven births, people remember the friendship of those who wiped away their sorrow.
